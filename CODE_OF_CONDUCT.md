# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and patient. Welcome contributors across languages and experience levels. Critique behavior and technical choices rather than people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Use public issues for technical work and keep customer data out of discussions. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's enabled private reporting channel described in SECURITY.md; do not reproduce harmful or private material publicly. If unavailable, request a private contact channel without naming affected people or disclosing details.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, warn contributors, restrict participation, or ban repeated/severe violations. They should consider context, explain decisions privately where appropriate, and protect reporter confidentiality. This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces.
